Quality and data controls

Master service catalogue

Sector tagging

Sector recommendations use the controlled names above. Where a sector brief uses a descriptive phrase such as “grant reporting”, “payroll support” or “regulatory compliance”, the related controlled service tag is selected and the detail is captured in Client Notes.

Operational workflow rules

Automations created when a client is saved are visible against that client record. These rules are ready for conversion to server-side CRM jobs and task assignments.

PIE / ISA 220 quality control

For a PIE, Financial Services or Public Sector client, create an open task for CPA Alarcon: “Assign Independent Engagement Quality Reviewer (CPA Kebeno Maonga Douglas).” An audit report cannot be signed until the task is complete.

Cross-selling follow-up

When Statutory Audit is selected and the audit is signed, create a day-30 account-manager task to offer Tax Diagnostic Reviews or Cash-flow Forecasting & Financial Modeling.

Tax compliance work packs

Tax Compliance now creates only the selected VAT and eTIMS, payroll and statutory, WHT, tax-credit, income-tax, PPE, stock, instalment-tax and client-report tasks. Credits or tax prepayments remain blocked from the tax position until iTax verification and reviewer approval are recorded.

Branch routing

New leads are routed to the Branch Manager role for Nairobi, Bungoma or Kisii. “Other” is routed to the Practice Administration queue for allocation.

Quarterly data clean-up

Review potential duplicates, incomplete profiles, client notes and engagement-letter references before each quarterly pipeline and quality-control review.
