STAFF TESTING ONLY — use fictional records, not real client or staff data.

This is a browser-local preview. Records stay on this browser/device and are not shared with colleagues. The staff-category selector is a testing control, not a secure login.

Open the staff testing checklist
  1. Select Engagement Partner or Manager in My Workspace. In Clients → Bulk staff and client import, upload a spreadsheet containing fictional records. Check mapped columns, duplicate counts and the import confirmation.
  2. Import a fictional client with only a name or Client ID. It should save as a Draft, with missing details in red. It must not activate an engagement or grant KYC approval.
  3. Click the saved client name, fill missing details and select Save client changes. Confirm the same record is updated, with no duplicate. Reopen it to check the changes.
  4. Open Time & HR → Team & HR. Create a fictional staff profile, click its name, change its category or employment details and select Save staff changes. The Staff ID and existing links should remain unchanged.
  5. Repeat the edit and import checks as Manager. Switch to Supervisor, Assistant and Intern: bulk import must disappear and master-profile saves must be blocked. This checks preview behaviour only, not production security.
  6. Record each result as Pass / Fail / Blocked, with the screen, selected category, steps, expected result and actual result. Share findings with your Manager through your normal channel; this preview does not send them automatically.

Not ready for live operations: real logins/setup links, shared records, secure permissions, background notifications, external payments and document retention require backend implementation and separate testing. Do not use preview invoices, approvals or filing states as official records.

Test only the checklist above for this release. Use a dedicated test browser profile to keep sample records separate from any existing local records. Do not clear or overwrite existing browser data.

    Master service catalogue

    These controlled terms are the only selectable service tags. They keep pipeline, service delivery and cross-selling reports consistent across the firm.

    Sector tagging

    Sector recommendations use the controlled names above. Where a sector brief uses a descriptive phrase such as “grant reporting”, “payroll support” or “regulatory compliance”, the related controlled service tag is selected and the detail is captured in Client Notes.

    Operational workflow rules

    Automations created when a client is saved are visible against that client record. These rules are ready for conversion to server-side CRM jobs and task assignments.

    Lead qualification and opportunity control

    Cold, Warm and Hot are priority signals, not compliance stages. Warm and Hot leads require an owner, next activity and due date. A qualified lead becomes an Opportunity when its proposal is sent; the opportunity retains the selected firm, routing and fee forecast.

    Lead-to-Engagement acceptance gate

    An opportunity cannot become an active engagement until the signed Engagement Letter reference is present, KYC documents are recorded and Manager-verified, the conflict check is cleared and all required approvals are complete. Statutory Audit also requires an ICPAK reference. Billing milestones are scheduled at activation, not issued merely because an opportunity is won.

    PIE / ISA 220 quality control

    For a PIE, Financial Services or Public Sector client, create an open task for the Engagement Partner: “Assign Independent Engagement Quality Reviewer.” An audit report cannot be signed until the assignment is complete.

    Controlled organisational routing

    Firm is the legal owner, Branch is the delivery office, Service Line is Audit, Tax, Accounting or Advisory, and the Specialist Team is the work queue. New leads route from Firm → Branch → Service Line → Specialist Team. A changed firm requires a new lead and engagement.

    Contract renewal and EL preservation

    Annual audits and recurring retainers alert the assigned owner 60 and 30 days before expiry. Starting a renewal creates a separate opportunity, refreshes KYC and conflict controls, and requires a new signed EL. The prior signed EL remains locked.

    Cross-selling follow-up

    When Statutory Audit is selected and the audit is signed, create a day-30 Account Manager task to offer Tax Diagnostic Reviews or Cash-flow Forecasting & Financial Modeling.

    Tax compliance work packs

    Tax Compliance now creates only the selected VAT and eTIMS, payroll and statutory, WHT, tax-credit, income-tax, PPE, stock, instalment-tax and client-report tasks. Credits or tax prepayments remain blocked from the tax position until iTax verification and reviewer approval are recorded.

    Quarterly data clean-up

    Review potential duplicates, incomplete profiles, client notes and engagement-letter references before each quarterly pipeline and quality-control review.

    Access control

    Permissions are assigned to positions and profiles, never embedded against a named individual. Actual users inherit access from their assigned position and reporting line.

    The current browser-local module displays the approved access model. Production deployment must enforce the same model in authenticated server-side permissions, not only in the browser interface.
