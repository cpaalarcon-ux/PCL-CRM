# Build 2905 release candidate

Staging browser UAT and the hosted smoke check passed on Build 2905. The local production web build passed. Signed-in acceptance is pending.

### Client-facing changes

- Messenger opens over the ERP and preserves your place and drafts. Send photo and document attachments, retry failed uploads, and share tasks and documents in a conversation.
- Resume saved drafts for opening balances, purchase and expenditure mapping, and time entry. A fixed navigation bar keeps Back, the page trail, and the current status in view.
- Review journals from a work queue and check, return, or approve selected entries together.
- Use the Non-eTIMS legal-bases shortcut from Purchases and follow the updated purchases import guidance.
- Prepare draft fixed-asset balances from the opening trial balance and add assets from purchase records.
