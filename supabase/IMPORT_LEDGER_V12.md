# Recognised imports, sales totals and purchase ledgers — testing release

28 October 2026. This combined staging-source update aligns purchase uploads, client opening balances, purchase mapping, recurring tasks and recurring engagement fees. Source publication does not apply database migrations or prove that the shared testing workspace has deployed the change. Apply the migrations through the staging release process and verify the Pages build before staff use these updated steps.

## Staff workflow

### Before any project or task

Set up the client, authorised handling firm, legal entity, supporting identity source and financial year-end. Then use **Clients → Opening balances** to download and fill the six-column A–F opening TB template, or record a reasoned confirmation that the client has no opening balances. The template has no extra G–I fields or Excel table; the routing category fills from the selected ERP account code. The TB file is a private **Import Source**, not evidence and not an accounting entry. Create the project for the confirmed client year only after that step. Once the project year exists, import the staged TB into an **opening draft journal** for validation and independent review; posting remains a separate approval step. If the file is wrong, remove it from active mapping and retain its archive history.

Open **Projects & tasks → monthly work project / service project** for purchase uploads and mapping.

- **Sales totals & payroll imports:** choose an uploaded CSV, XLSX or ZIP and the reporting period. Recognised KRA sales sections are aggregated into 16%, 8%, zero-rated and exempt totals. No individual sales invoice records are created. Source files are import evidence, not separate completed working papers. A totals-only source must state whether credits are already included. Separate credit reductions are applied only when the source is before credits.
- **Standard purchase upload:** use the 14-column A–N template for all purchase classifications. Row 1 is checked and ignored; transactions begin on row 2. Net, VAT and gross are fixed in H, I and J. For credit notes, K and L are the original invoice number and date; leave them blank for ordinary invoices. M is the ERP routing category code and N is the ERP TB item code. The template’s account list provides dropdown choices. If both codes are valid and match an active ERP account, the ERP suggests the mapping for staff confirmation; it never classifies or posts the row automatically. Leave both M/N blank when unknown and use the ERP’s mapping lists. Choose the client, one VAT category (16%, 8%, 0% or Exempt) and the client reporting month, then check rows and totals before import. Zero-rated and exempt purchases require separate uploads because their monetary values alone cannot distinguish them. Source amounts are preserved and rounded to two decimals using controlled rounding; rounded gross must equal rounded net plus VAT. Blank rows are skipped. The 10,000-row limit counts populated purchase rows. Invalid dates, missing VAT and inconsistent totals identify the source row and block that row from import. Missing credit-note original references/dates can instead be retained as Pending information after explicit acknowledgement. Older monthly-section imports retain specific range-error messages.
- **Classify purchase imports:** recognised KRA sections and supported workbook templates supply the columns automatically. Inspect every source row by scrolling. Filter costs by VAT category and map selected rows, or all filtered rows, into goods for resale, construction raw materials with job/site, a detailed expense account, or PPE. Accounting classification remains separate from VAT review.
- **Purchase ledgers:** scroll through all matching transactions and filter by classification, VAT category, date or reference. Detailed source net, VAT and gross amounts, review/posting status and import references remain visible. Excel exports retain Arial 9, wrap text and no frozen panes. Missing VAT is displayed as missing and blocks accounting posting until confirmed.
- **Payroll:** supported payroll breakdown and 25-column PAYE layouts retain employee source figures, including missing details. ERP calculations use the existing dated approved/testing rules. Residence must be confirmed when absent from the source. Differences and derived amounts are displayed; the preparer must confirm using calculated values and explain differences. A separate Supervisor/Manager/Partner confirms the import. It can then populate the PAYE computation and prepare a payroll accounting entry. Gross cash pay plus employer contributions is expensed once; employee deductions and settlements use liabilities.
- **Client accounting & profit:** independently confirmed sales/payroll imports prepare balanced drafts. A different Manager/Partner posts them. Repeated preparation returns the same journal. Only posted entries feed the TB and profit. PPE continues through the existing asset-class, shared-rate and independent approval workflow.
- **Recurring engagement fees:** use **Billing & WIP → Recurring fees** to set the project-linked description, agreed amount before VAT, VAT rate, monthly/quarterly/annual frequency and date range. A Partner can create due invoice drafts; a Manager/Partner can pause, resume or cancel future drafts. Existing invoices are unchanged, drafts are never posted or sent by the schedule, and this fee cycle is separate from recurring project tasks.

## Optional cells and pending credit information

Staff leave optional cells blank; N/A is not required. G (Customs entry number) is optional for local purchases. K/L are left blank for ordinary invoices and completed for credit notes. M/N may both be blank; if used, both must identify a matching active ERP account. N/A, NA, Not applicable and dashes in optional fields are treated as missing, while raw source values are retained. H/I/J require actual numeric values; missing amounts are never silently changed to zero. Excel serial dates are displayed as DD/MM/YYYY without changing stored source values or invoice references.

The check screen separates whole-file totals, complete-row totals and signed pending-credit totals. Whole-file totals are unavailable if any amount cannot be read. Staff explicitly confirm importing complete rows and retaining incomplete credits. Pending credits remain immutable source exceptions, visible with file, row, credit reference, amounts and a source download. They have no posting-source row and cannot be mapped or posted. Purchase ledger screens/exports and accounting reports disclose their excluded totals/counts. VAT and annual tax submission remain blocked until the required original details are supplied; a generic reconciliation note cannot waive this.

To complete a pending credit, correct K/L in the original file and upload it to the same project and reporting period. Existing complete rows are skipped; newly complete credits enter normal classification and independent review. The pending item clears only when the credit identity, amounts, reporting period and other source fields match a completed imported row. Historical source exceptions remain retained. Repeated pending snapshots do not inflate the outstanding totals. Imports into submitted/locked tax periods are rejected. No database schema change is required.

## Purchase mapping dropdowns

The existing mapping screen keeps business activity, inventory use, product/material category and detailed account together. Product categories come from the client category register, accessible stock controls and current independently reviewed mappings. A Manager/Partner can add a category inline for the selected activity/use, or client-wide when no activity is selected. This creates an audited category definition only; it does not create a stock balance or classify any source row. Missing categories are explained and a required missing category prevents submission. Common narration stays optional, with each original narration retained when blank.

Account choices are filtered by inventory use and by the method effective on every selected reporting date. Goods for resale excludes work in progress, production labour/overheads and cost-of-sales accounts, while retaining supported purchase, merchandise, transit and landed-cost accounts. Construction continues to require its raw-material account, material category and job/site. If a selection crosses Periodic/Perpetual policies, it must be mapped in separate groups. API checks enforce the compatible accounts, activity dates and configured category contract for the new screen. Existing legacy mapping API clients retain their text-category compatibility.

The client category register has forced row-level security, Manager/Partner-only inserts, client/activity consistency checks and immutable audit history. No existing client category, policy or purchase row is automatically changed. The new migration is `purchase_mapping_stock_categories`. Saving this mapping screen refreshes its purchase list without reloading the full workspace. Rows remain subject to independent review and posting.

Browser checks cover desktop/mobile dropdowns, inline category creation, stale category clearing after inventory-use changes, the confirmation message and absence of a full-workspace request after mapping. Database tests cover scoped category management, denied Assistant creation, duplicate category reuse, category validation and unchanged source amounts/narrations.

## History and duplicate controls

The compact purchase upload uses the ten source columns A–J followed only by K: Original invoice number and L: Original invoice date. Both fields are recognised across B02A–B02D and headered CSV/XLSX purchase layouts. Ordinary invoice rows may leave them blank. Credit-note dates and references remain in the existing document columns, with signed negative net/VAT amounts. Original invoice dates can precede the selected reporting period, but invalid dates and dates after the current document are rejected. KRA F/G imports also retain the supplied original date from column 11.

On the standard 14-column template, M/N are validated mapping suggestions, while K/L hold credit-note original invoice references. On older multi-tab/KRA purchase workbooks, legacy routing/classification columns are not imported as approved mappings; staff classify rows using the ERP dropdowns. In every format the source file remains unchanged, a suggestion needs staff confirmation, and existing ERP mappings are not overwritten.

Sales summaries are period snapshots. A replacement must fully cover and explicitly identify every overlapping prior version. Independent confirmation atomically retires the old coverage while preserving its history. Daily database reservations prevent concurrent or hidden overlapping confirmed summaries for the same client, including another handling project. Weekly and monthly totals cannot be added over the same dates. Existing invoice-based sales are not silently migrated: overlapping legacy records block summary import pending reconciliation.

Drafts and submitted summaries cannot alter stored source payloads. Corrections use replacement snapshots. A summary already linked to an accounting journal must have its linked reversal independently posted before replacement. Existing draft journals must be resolved first. Locked tax periods remain protected. Purchase credits retain signed amounts and original references. Unmatched purchase credits, or cumulative reductions above the original net/VAT, are blocked from posting. Missing originals need supported import and review.

## Format scope

Verified supplied KRA contracts: B general sales, D1 zero-rated sales, E exempt sales, F general purchases, H zero-rated purchases and I exempt purchases; historical C/G contracts retain an 8% review flag. Exact layout, populated dates and amount positions are checked, not just filenames. Generic invoice exports require explicit recognised headers. Unknown or changed layouts are retained for review; they do not silently become sales or amounts of zero. KRA sections with a formula-derived VAT field use the VAT-exclusive amount and mark the derived value. Raw source values are retained.

Payroll summaries and footer blocks are excluded from employee counts. Missing employee PINs, unsupported deductions/reliefs and unavailable dated rules remain exceptions. Insurance relief requiring a premium/evidence calculation is handled in the existing detailed tax computation rather than inferred from a relief amount. This release does not add employee-master monthly roll-forward, a certified KRA filing export, full stock issue costing or automatic financial-year closure.
